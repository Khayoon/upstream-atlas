UPSTREAM — ONTARIO: DATA AND THIRD-PARTY NOTICES

This interactive atlas incorporates a transformed regional subset of HydroRIVERS
version 1.0 and Natural Earth geography. River data have been clipped to Ontario,
reprojected, simplified, routed and annotated for this visualization. River-system
colors, presentation and calculated network distances are project transformations.
The upstream providers have not endorsed or independently verified those changes.
The data files are components of this atlas, not a distribution of the original
continental source datasets.

TORONTO WATERSHED BOUNDARIES — TRCA

The Humber, Don and Rouge watershed boundaries are derived from TRCA's
Watersheds_TRCA polygon layer, reprojected for the atlas. These are complete
watershed polygons, not buffers constructed from the displayed stream lines.
https://services1.arcgis.com/d0ZCwU7eGKVeNiEE/arcgis/rest/services/Watersheds_TRCA/FeatureServer/0
